A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity, with something to build.

I’m a Hmong American computer science student from Minnesota. I enjoy getting past “it works” and understanding why it works—from the responsibilities of a Spring service to the way a database keeps a catch record around after a restart.

FishingLog brings that curiosity together with something I enjoy outside of school: fishing. Building a tool I’d use myself gives me a reason to keep learning and a practical way to put those lessons to work.

Alongside school, I’ve worked in restaurant service, trained coworkers, and coordinated busy shifts. Those experiences have taught me to communicate clearly, stay dependable, and follow through when people are counting on me.
